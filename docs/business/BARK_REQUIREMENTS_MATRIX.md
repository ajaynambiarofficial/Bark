# Bark requirements and traceability matrix

**Version:** 1.0 draft  
**Assessment date:** 15 November 2026  
**Status:** Proposed requirements are subject to management approval. Implementation status reflects repository evidence, not a production certification.

## Status key

- **Implemented and verified:** code exists and direct relevant verification is documented.
- **Implemented but not verified:** code is present; complete workflow/integration evidence is absent.
- **Partially implemented:** only some steps or constraints exist.
- **Frontend-only or mocked:** local/fixture UI without operational persistence or delivery.
- **Planned but not implemented:** proposed requirement not functionally present.
- **Unknown / needs decision:** policy or source of truth needs an owner decision.

## 2. Access-control overview

The table below is a proposed business-readable summary of seeded backend permissions, not a substitute for validating clinic assignment and row-level behavior. **S** = own/self scope, **C** = assigned clinic or team scope, **G** = broader/global scope, **A** = administrator configuration, **—** = no seeded action. Super Admin has all permission keys. Actual access also depends on the user's active account, assigned clinics, clearance, and explicit user grants/denials.

| Role | Dashboard | Create/view bookings | Update/cancel/assign | Escalations | Calendar | Admin controls |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sales/CX Agent | S | Create; view permitted records | — | Create/view/comment | View | — |
| Clinic Front Desk | S | Create; view permitted records | Update; no seeded cancel | Create/view/comment | View | — |
| Doctor/Veterinarian | S | View | — | View/comment | View | — |
| Groomer | S | View | — | Create/view/comment | View | — |
| Sales Team Lead | S/C team | Create/view | Update/cancel | Assign; Tier 2; close; create/comment | Manage | — |
| Operations/Sales Manager | S/C team | Create/view; manage-any booking | Update/cancel | Assign; Tier 2/3; close; create/comment | Manage | — |
| Clinic Manager | S/C clinic | Create/view; manage-any booking within access rules | Update/cancel | Assign; Tier 2/3; close; create/comment | Manage | — |
| Zonal Head | S/C team | Create/view permitted records | Update/cancel | Assign; Tier 2/3; close; create/comment | Manage | — |
| Central Management | G | View/manage-any | Manage-any | Manage-any | Manage | — |
| System Administrator | G | View only through common grants | — | View/comment through common grants | View | Users, roles, workflows, settings, data, audit, emergency |
| GOD / Super Admin | G | All | All | All | All | All |

“Operations Manager” is not a separate seeded key; the code has Sales Manager, Clinic Manager, Central Management, and Zonal Head. Management must approve whether a distinct role is required and how the prompt's Team Lead/Manager/Zonal visibility is represented.

## 3. Requirement traceability notes

- IDs beginning `BR-FR` map business needs to source and acceptance criteria.
- “Implemented” means evidence in source, not necessarily tested for operational safety.
- External data imported into a display is not automatically a supported integration or source of truth.
- The backend may support an operation that the current frontend does not expose; the acceptance condition requires the role-appropriate employee workflow as well as the API.
